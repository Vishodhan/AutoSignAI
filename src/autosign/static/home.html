Get your client agreement signed on every file

Upload the agreement (as a PDF) that every new client must sign. AutoSign finds where your company signs and where the client signs, even in an agreement it has never seen. For each client, DocuSeal puts your company's signature on, you check it, and DocuSeal emails the client to sign.

  1. Enter your details and upload your agreement as a PDF, once. AutoSign writes your name in cursive: that is your company's signature.
  2. Check the signature places AutoSign found. It asks you to confirm whenever it isn't sure.
  3. Add a client (name and email). DocuSeal signs the client's copy for your company, with today's date, and you check it.
  4. Send it. DocuSeal emails the client, who signs on DocuSeal's page.
  5. Get the signed PDF: DocuSeal emails it to you and the client, and the dashboard shows where every client is.
