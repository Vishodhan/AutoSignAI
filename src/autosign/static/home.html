Get your client agreement signed on every file

Upload the agreement (as a PDF) that every new client must sign. AutoSign finds where your company signs and where the client signs, even in an agreement it has never seen, and signs it for your company. Then, for each new client file, you copy a signing link and send it. The client signs in the browser, and you get the signed PDF.

  1. Upload your agreement as a PDF, once.
  2. Check the signature places AutoSign found. It asks you to confirm whenever it isn't sure.
  3. AutoSign signs for your company, once, on the copy every client will sign.
  4. Open a client file (one per client case) and copy its signing link to the client.
  5. The client signs; the signed PDF and the audit log are on your agreement page.
